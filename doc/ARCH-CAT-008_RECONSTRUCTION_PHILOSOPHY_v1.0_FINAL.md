# ARCH-CAT-008 Reconstruction Philosophy

**Version:** 1.0 REVIEW DRAFT
**Status:** Proposed freeze before visual reconstruction

## Decision

SuiSakhi shall use a hybrid reconstruction architecture combining visual
analysis and structural design understanding.

## Frozen principles

1. Generated views are never treated as observed truth.
2. Every generated view carries provenance.
3. Every generated view requires human review before approval.
4. Visual consistency takes precedence over creativity.
5. Catalogue imagery must not produce physical measurements.
6. Customer Reference Designs remain outside automated Catalogue conversion.
7. Approved Design Representation and Material Representation remain separate.
8. Provider-specific technology remains behind the provider adapter contract.
9. Original assets remain immutable.
10. Rejected and superseded generated revisions remain auditable.
