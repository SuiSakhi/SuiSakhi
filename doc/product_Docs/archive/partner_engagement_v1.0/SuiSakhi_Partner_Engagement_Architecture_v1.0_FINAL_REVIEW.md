# SuiSakhi Partner Engagement Architecture v1.0 FINAL REVIEW

## Status
Final Consolidated Review Version

## 1. Vision & Core Principles
- Simple onboarding
- Admin-assisted completion
- Metadata-driven governance
- Partner acceptance before payment
- Customer trust and Partner trust equally important

## 2. SuiSakhi Trust Promise
"If something goes wrong, we can identify what happened, protect the customer, communicate truthfully, recover the work where possible, treat the Partner fairly, and preserve an auditable record."

## 3. Phase-1 Partner Categories
1. Tailor Partner
2. Designer Partner
3. Measurement Partner
4. Doorstep / Quick-Fix Partner
5. Boutique Partner
6. Laundry Partner
7. Pressing Partner
8. Delivery Partner
9. Brand Partner

## 4. Roles
### Super Admin
Governance, metadata, security, approvals, audit, commercial policy.

### Admin
Partner relationship owner, onboarding, gap analysis, KYC, operations.

### Verification/KYC
Identity, document, address, business verification.

### Commercial
Rates, commercial negotiations, packages, promotions.

### Partner Operations
Capacity, assignment interventions, SLA, incidents.

### Helpdesk
Order/service support only.

### Chatbot
L0 support and ticket intake.

## 5. Visibility Model
### Customer 360
Profiles, measurements, orders, subscriptions, tickets, payments.

### Partner 360
Business, capabilities, rates, KYC, assignments, capacity, incidents, catalog.

### Service/Order 360
Restricted Helpdesk view.

## 6. Partner Onboarding
Basic Details -> Business & Operations -> Category Details -> Commercials -> Agreement -> Submit

Actions:
- Save Draft
- Accept & Submit for Review

## 7. Admin Assisted Gap Analysis
Partner Draft -> Admin Review -> Missing Information -> Partner Clarification -> Admin Completion -> Verification

- KYC Verified
- Capabilities Configured
- Service Area Configured
- Availability Configured
- Capacity Configured
- Rate Card Ready
- Settlement Ready
- Notifications Enabled

## 18. Availability & Capacity
Available, Busy, Paused, Emergency Stop, Suspended.

## 19. Assignment Framework
Customer Request -> Assignment -> Partner Acceptance -> Capacity Reservation -> Payment -> Execution

Rule: No payment before Partner acceptance.

## 20. Pending Assignment Queue
Admin monitors assignment failures, capacity issues and peak-season intervention.

## 21. SLA Framework
Every order snapshots SLA version.

Never hide a delay until the promised date has passed.

## 22. Custody Framework
Garments, fabric, samples, accessories tracked through custody events.

## 23. Loss & Damage
Incident -> Evidence -> Assessment -> Resolution -> Compensation -> Audit.

No silent deletion of evidence.

## 24. Complaint & Dispute Framework
Chatbot -> Helpdesk -> Admin -> Resolution.

## 25. Rework Governance
Defect analysis, responsibility attribution, customer communication.

## 26. Partner DNA Reports
### Tailor DNA
Measurement version, machine, needle, thread, QC.

### Laundry DNA
Wash method, chemical, temperature, QC.

### Pressing DNA
Pressing method, temperature, steam, QC.

## 27. Fair Partner Governance
No Partner is penalized solely because of one rating, decline or complaint.

## 28. Reason Codes
Cancellation, Delay, Decline, Rework, Damage, Loss, Capacity, Emergency, etc.

## 29. Settlement & Ledger
Partner cost, platform fee, incentive, penalty, settlement amount.

## 30. Helpdesk & Chatbot Rules
Helpdesk cannot:
- Approve Partners
- Perform KYC
- View bank details
- Change metadata
- Override financial controls

## 31. Security & Privacy
Least privilege, masking, audit, consent, retention.

## 32. Phase-1 Scope
Core partner ecosystem, onboarding, KYC, commercials, assignment, SLA, disputes, metadata.

## 33. Final Operating Principle
Build trust for Customers and Partners through transparency, governance, recoverability and auditability.
